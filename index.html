오아영 채널 도구 (Oayeong Channel Tool)

An internal tool that helps one YouTube channel publish subtitles, titles and descriptions in 14 languages, and sends the channel owner a weekly report on how the channel is doing.

The Korean name, 오아영 채널 도구, is the app name shown on the Google sign-in (consent) screen.

Internal tool, not offered to anyone else. There is no sign-up, download, app or web service. The tool is a set of command-line scripts that the channel's editor (the operator) runs on one Windows PC, for one channel only.

The channel

The tool works on one channel: 촉이 오아영 ChokA0 (channel ID UCvHOjrz1843NFK7rkyPQ2ug), a Korean vlog channel. Korean subtitles are part of the video image, so the tool adds subtitle tracks for viewers who do not speak Korean.

Who authorized it

The channel owner signed in with her own Google account and authorized the tool. She is the only person who has authorized it. The tool's setup step confirms that the signed-in channel is the channel above. The channel owner can remove the tool's access at any time on the Google security settings page.

What the tool does

  1. Subtitles. Adds and replaces caption tracks in 14 languages (en, ja, zh-Hant, zh-Hans, de, fr, es-ES, es-419, it, th, id, pt-BR, ar, tr) on the channel's own videos.
  2. Translated titles and descriptions. Adds the translated title and description of each of the channel's own videos for the same languages.
  3. Weekly report. Builds a report for the channel owner from the channel's YouTube Analytics and YouTube Reporting data, and the public view, like, comment and subscriber counts of the channel and its videos. The report is emailed only to the channel owner and the operator.

The tool uses YouTube API Services: the YouTube Data API, the YouTube Analytics API and the YouTube Reporting API.

What it does not do

  • It does not upload videos and does not search YouTube.
  • It does not act on any other channel.
  • It shows no ads and does not sell, rent or trade any data.

Data

What the tool collects, why, where it is kept and when it is deleted is described in the Privacy Policy. In short: analytics data and statistics are kept only in a local folder on the operator's PC and are not synced to cloud storage. The tool does not collect comment text or data about other channels.
